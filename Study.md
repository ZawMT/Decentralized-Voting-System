## Study note

### What the project is about?
A decentralized voting application built with a Solidity smart contract on Ethereum, a Python/FastAPI backend, and a JavaScript/Web3 frontend.

### What is the purpose of the project?
To demonstrate how blockchain can make voting tamper-proof and transparent, removing the need for a central authority to count or validate votes.
